# Model evaluation report

Generated by `churn evaluate`. Promoted model: `lgbm-20250630-29a8eca6`.

## Label, window and splits

- **Label (A1):** a subscriber is a churner when they have a voluntary cancellation within 30 days after the scoring date. Involuntary cancellations (payment failure) are not positives.
- **Window:** 30 days from each scoring date.
- **Synthetic scale:** small.
- **Training:** monthly snapshots 2023-09-30 to 2024-09-30 (13 snapshots, 120309 rows). The evaluation model is fitted on these only and is not registered.
- **Validation:** the 3 months before the holdout, 2024-11-30 to 2024-12-31 (2 snapshots after the purge, 32638 rows, 969 churn labels). Used only to choose the at-risk boundary.
- **Holdout:** time-based, the last 3 months of monthly snapshots: 2025-02-28 to 2025-04-30. It is never seen in training or boundary selection.
- **Purge:** snapshots whose 30-day label window reaches the next split are dropped: 2024-10-31, 2025-01-31.
- **Holdout size:** 55536 scored (snapshot, subscriber) rows, 1700 churn labels (base rate 3.1%).

## Synthetic data and the impact target

Synthetic accuracy is not real-world accuracy: the data is generated by this repository, so these numbers say nothing about real Thesis customers. The 90-day impact target can only be checked after go-live.

## At-risk boundary and recall

- **Recommended at-risk boundary:** 0.030, the highest threshold whose recall on the validation split is at least 0.65 (validation recall 71.2% over 969 validation churners). It is a recommendation only: the runtime tiers and `AGENT_CONFIG` are not changed by this report.
- **Holdout recall at the boundary:** 62.9%: 1120 of 1781 cancelling churners caught, 64 with no score before cancelling, 597 with a last score below the boundary.
- **Holdout precision at the boundary (reported, no target):** 10.3%: 1115 churners among 10792 rows scored at or above the boundary.
- Pre-cancellation recall counts a churner as caught when their last holdout score before cancelling was at or above the boundary. Churners with no score before cancelling count as missed.

## Precision ceilings

Computed from the measured holdout base rate b, not from an assumed rate. A perfect model puts every churner at the top, so:

| Ceiling | Formula | Value at measured b |
|---|---|---|
| Top-decile precision | min(1, b/0.10) | 30.6% |
| Top-decile lift | min(10, 1/b) | 10.0x |
| Top-2% precision | min(1, b/0.02) | 100.0% |

**Why the 0.70 top-decile target was replaced:** under this label (A1) a perfect model's top-decile precision is at most b/0.10, so a target of 0.70 is only reachable when b is at least 7%. At the measured base rate of 3.1% the ceiling is 30.6%. The PRD (Revision 2) replaced it with top-2% precision > 0.50 and top-decile lift >= 4x, which have reachable ceilings. The earlier measurement (T8, fixed 0.20 boundary) was an honest MISS.
