# T12 — Guardrails and coverage gaps

- Feature: churn-prediction
- Ticket: T12 (issue #12)
- Branch: `ticket/T12`
- Covers: R4.6, R6.5 (gap logging), PRD §7 edge cases (unsubscribed, active offer, pending approval, frequency cap).

## What was built

`agent.guardrails` drops arms before the policy, using the run's pinned `GuardrailConfig`:

- Contact cap: 1 in 14 days or 3 in 90 days, counted from `INTERVENTION_LOG` rows with `execution_status` `DISPATCHED` or `PENDING_APPROVAL`. The window ends at the score's `as_of`. The decision being executed is not counted against itself. Over the cap, every arm is removed (`cap`).
- Email unsubscribe (`email_unsubscribed` from the point-in-time features) removes email arms only. Escalation stays.
- An outstanding `retention_offer` (dispatched or pending at or before `as_of`, outcome not yet retained/churned) removes new offers (`active_offer`).
- The offer arm is removed when no catalogue code is at or under `max_discount_pct` (`discount`). When a legal code exists, `RetentionOffer` keeps a discount within the cap.
- An unprocessed `APPROVALS` row with `decision = PENDING` and `created_at <= as_of` removes every arm (`DUPLICATE_PENDING`).

Each removal is a `guardrail_events` entry (`event=REMOVED`, `arm`, `reason`) on the decision and in the rationale's `removed` list. When no arm remains, `run_agent` writes one `COVERAGE_GAPS` row for `(run_id, subscriber_key)` and does not return a decision. `run_agent` also copies `max_discount_pct` and `offer_catalogue` from the pinned guardrails onto the context.

`execution.approvals.process` re-checks an `APPROVED` row for the cap and for email unsubscribe. A failure is `CANCELLED_STALE` with that reason and no outbox row. Unsubscribe is read from features built at processing time, so an unsubscribe after scoring still cancels an email. The cap stays anchored at the decision's score `as_of`.

## Deviations

- The migration is `V017__coverage_gaps.sql`, not `V007__coverage_gaps.sql`. `V007__risk_scores_archetype.sql` has already shipped, and the highest migration on main was `V016`. The next free version is `V017`.
- `execution/approvals.py` is not on the T12 file list. ARCHITECTURE §6.5 (operator decision during T14) says this ticket adds the contact-cap and email-unsubscribe re-check there. The check is one call into `blocked_approvals`.
- `InterventionType.eligible(context)` was not added. `interventions/base.py` belongs to T26 in this same wave. The global rules live in `guardrails.py`.
- Holdout, budget, and agent-halt gap reasons are not written. Holdout is T16. A halt gap would mean editing `runner.py`, which this ticket does not touch. A critical low-confidence subscriber whose escalation arm was removed becomes a coverage gap instead of `select` raising.

## Proposed ARCHITECTURE notes

- §5.3: `COVERAGE_GAPS` shipped as `V017__coverage_gaps.sql` (`run_id`, `subscriber_key`, `reason`, `guardrail_events`, `created_at`). `V007` is the archetype columns and was not reused.
- §6.4: contact counts and the outstanding-offer and pending-approval checks are point-in-time at the score `as_of`. A row timestamped after `as_of` is not visible to that decision. Gap reasons written by T12 are `cap`, `unsubscribed+no channel`, `active_offer`, `discount`, and `DUPLICATE_PENDING`. `holdout`, `halted`, and `budget` are still later tickets.
- §6.4: an offer whose every catalogue code is above `max_discount_pct` is removed. A catalogue that still has a legal code is left to `RetentionOffer`, which clips to the cap. The cap and the catalogue on the context come from the pinned guardrail snapshot.
- §6.4: per-type `eligible(context)` is not on `InterventionType`. T26 owns `base.py`.
- §6.5: the `APPROVED` re-check now includes the contact cap (same `as_of` window, excluding the decision itself) and email unsubscribe (features at processing time). Either failure is `CANCELLED_STALE` with reason `cap` or `unsubscribed`.
