## What was built

`evaluate` adds a data-provenance section to the report: the eight mapped contract views, the synth scale, the customer count, the seed, the training / validation / holdout row counts, and the share of holdout rows with each `has_<domain> = 1` and with `orders_total > 0`.

`docs/eval-report.md` and `docs/model-card.md` were regenerated from that code. The model card still says synthetic accuracy is not real-world accuracy and that the 90-day impact target can only be checked after go-live.

## Deviations

- `small` (30,000 customers) produced 53,056 holdout rows. T8's committed holdout was 55,536, and the acceptance test requires at least that many. The committed rerun uses scale name `eval` at 36,000 customers (seed 20250630), which is the same generator and a larger draw. That name is not in `config/settings.yaml`. Reproduce with `settings.synth.scales["eval"] = 36000`, then `generate`, `train`, and `evaluate(..., scale="eval")`.
- T8 and T29 are on main. This ticket did not wait for T27. The draw is under the 40,000-customer chunk threshold, so it is one shot either way.
