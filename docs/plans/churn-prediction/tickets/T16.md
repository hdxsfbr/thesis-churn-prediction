# T16 — Holdout, simulated responses and outcome tracking

- Feature: churn-prediction
- Ticket: T16 (issue #16)
- Branch: `ticket/T16`
- Covers: R6.2, R6.6.

## What was built

`feedback.holdout` assigns each at-risk subscriber to control or treatment by `sha256(salt|subscriber_key)` into 10,000 buckets. The default share is 0.10 and the default salt is `v1`. The first assignment sticks. Control members get an `INTERVENTION_LOG` row with `intervention_type = HOLDOUT` and no outbox row. Treatment members go through the existing guardrail, policy, content and gate path.

`feedback.outcomes` closes `DISPATCHED` and `HOLDOUT` rows once `dispatched_at + window_days` is at or before the clock. Response is `redeemed`, then `clicked`, then `opened`, then `none`. A redemption is a discounted order inside the response window for a `retention_offer`. Outcome is `churned` for a voluntary cancellation inside the window, `retained` when a subscription is still active at the window end, and `unknown` otherwise. The row is merged into `OUTCOMES` and copied onto the log.

`synth.responder` appends a simulated send, and sometimes an open, a click, a discounted order and a voluntary cancellation, for each new `READY` outbox row. Ids start with `sim-`. A second call does not write them again.

`churn simulate --days N` starts at `--as-of` (today when omitted) and, once per day, runs the responder and then closes elapsed outcomes. The cycle itself closes elapsed outcomes after dispatch. It does not call the responder.

`sql/reports/impact.sql` returns a churn rate for `treated` and for `control` (`HOLDOUT`). `unknown` is left out of both rates.

## Deviations

- The migration is `V019__outcomes_holdout.sql`. `V010` is already taken. `V018` was the highest shipped ops migration.
- The assignment column is `arm_group`, not `group`. `GROUP` is reserved, and the MERGE writer does not quote identifiers.
- A holdout row stores the decision time in `dispatched_at` so the 30-day window has a start. `approval_status` and `execution_status` are `HOLDOUT`. `content_source` is `template` because the model is not called. Those rows are not returned to `dispatch`.
- `RUNS.at_risk` is still the full at-risk count. T19 subtracts the holdout from the coverage denominator. This ticket does not write a `COVERAGE_GAPS` reason of `holdout`.
- The responder is not part of `run_cycle`. Outbox `created_at` is the wall clock, and the synthetic source ends on 2025-06-30, so `simulate` defaults the clock to today. A cancellation sets `cancel_reason_code = sim_responder` and a future `cancelled_at`, and leaves the subscription `loaded_at` unchanged.
- The tiny score has about 12 at-risk subscribers, so a 10% holdout often contains nobody. The share test uses 10,000 keys. The outbox test uses `k3` (control) and `k0` (treatment) for salt `v1`.
- `tests/integration/test_agent_thin_path.py`, `test_approval_lifecycle.py` and `test_outbox_idempotency.py` counted every at-risk subscriber as an actionable decision. They now leave `HOLDOUT` out of the registry, approval and queued-count checks. Those files are outside the slice. The suite fails if they stay as they were.
- A synthetic treated-versus-control rate is not the 90-day impact target. That target can only be checked after go-live.

## Proposed ARCHITECTURE notes

- Shipped migration is `V019`, not `V010`. `HOLDOUT_ASSIGNMENTS.arm_group` is `control` or `treatment`. The first assignment for a `subscriber_key` sticks.
- Control is removed before guardrails and policy. The log row is `HOLDOUT`, with `dispatched_at` equal to the decision time, and it is not dispatched. `RUNS.at_risk` is unchanged. T19 owns the coverage denominator `at_risk - holdout`.
- The cycle's `outcomes` step closes `DISPATCHED` and `HOLDOUT` rows through the contract views. Response priority is redeemed, clicked, opened, none. A redemption is `discount_usd > 0` on an order in the response window for `retention_offer`. Outcome is voluntary cancellation inside the window, else still active at the window end, else unknown.
- `churn simulate --days N` is the clock. It calls `synth.responder` and then `close_outcomes` once per day. The responder is not on the cycle path. Simulated ids are prefixed `sim-`.
- `sql/reports/impact.sql` reports treated and control churn rates and drops `unknown`.
